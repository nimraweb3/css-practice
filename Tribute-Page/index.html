About Waqar Zaka

Waqar Zaka is a Pakistani television personality, entrepreneur, technology enthusiast, and digital educator. After gaining popularity through television, he shifted his focus toward blockchain technology, cryptocurrency, artificial intelligence, and online education.

Over the years, he has introduced thousands of Pakistanis to blockchain, Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other emerging technologies. Through live streams, online sessions, and educational content, he encourages young people to learn valuable digital skills instead of simply consuming technology.

Waqar has also participated in public welfare campaigns, legal awareness, and disaster relief efforts. His mission is to promote innovation, financial literacy, and technological advancement across Pakistan.

Whether people agree with all of his views or not, his influence on spreading awareness of cryptocurrency and blockchain in Pakistan has made him one of the country's most recognizable voices in the tech community.
